NumPy Broadcasting Visualizer

Enter two shapes as NumPy would report them (e.g. 3,4 or 4,) and see which dimensions get stretched to make the operation work.

+

Rule: align the two shapes from the right. Two dimensions are compatible if they're equal, or if either one is 1 (it gets stretched/repeated to match) — a missing dimension on the shorter shape counts as a 1. If any aligned pair is neither equal nor 1, NumPy raises exactly the error shown above. Try 3,4 + 3, to see that failure — it's one dimension short of the 3,1 case that does work.
