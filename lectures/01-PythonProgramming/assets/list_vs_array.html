Lists vs. NumPy Arrays: Memory & Speed

Two costs of a plain Python list for numerical work: what it stores, and how it's laid out in memory. This is the same idea behind id(a[0]), id(a[1])... from the lecture — just drawn out.

The bars are an illustrative model (order of magnitude, not a real measurement) so the shape of the gap shows up even without running anything. The real numbers depend on your machine — measure them yourself in a notebook cell:
%timeit sum(x)  vs  %timeit sum(a) for a loop  vs  %timeit arr.sum().
Notice the numpy bar barely grows with N at first — that's fixed call overhead — then catches up once N is large enough that the actual arithmetic dominates.
